Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

1. What we collect

We collect your name, email address, and optionally your phone number when you join our waitlist or create an account. We also collect usage data such as rides taken, games played, and points earned to power your rewards experience.

2. How we use it

Your data is used to operate MetroSafar — to credit points, personalise content to your interests, send you app launch notifications, and improve the product. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data storage

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore). We comply with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023. Users under 18 require verifiable parental consent before their data is processed for behavioural features.

4. Cookies & analytics

This website does not use tracking cookies. The MetroSafar app uses Firebase Analytics to understand aggregate usage patterns. Analytics are disabled for users who have indicated they are under 18.

5. Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing privacy@metrosafar.in.

6. Contact

For any privacy-related questions: privacy@metrosafar.in
